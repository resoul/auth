    My passkeys
    
    
    
    
    
    
    Adding or deleting a key and new recovery codes ask for a fresh passkey proof (step-up); the
       last key cannot be deleted.
    
  
    

  
    
    Approve a device
    
    
    

    
    
    
    
    
    Approving asks for a fresh passkey proof (step-up) and the six digits the other device shows. Three wrong codes kill the request.
       The other device gets a session of its own; this one is not copied.
    
  
    

  
    
    My email
    
    
    

    
    
    
    
    Changing the email asks for a fresh passkey proof (step-up), mails a code to the new address and
       confirms with it; the old address is told after the change. A code cannot be used from another session.
    
  
    

  
    
    Delete account
    
    Deletes all sessions, passkeys, recovery codes and pending account mail. Audit events remain
       without account links for 90 days. A fresh passkey proof is required.
